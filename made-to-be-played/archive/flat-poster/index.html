Glass Street

Night in Hong Kong as a flat-colour poster, cut into sheets that slide against each other. Nothing here is a photo or an image file: it is all drawn in code, with the neon and the windows lit on top.

Move the pointer and the sheets slide against each other. Click the sign and the tubes stutter. Click the taxi and it flashes its lights. Scroll and the layers peel apart.
