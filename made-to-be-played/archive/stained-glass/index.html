Glass Street

Night in Hong Kong, built as sheets of leaded glass with a gap between each. Nothing here is a photo or an image file: it is all drawn in code, then lit from behind.

Move the pointer and the sheets slide against each other. Click the sign and the tubes stutter. Click the taxi and it flashes its lights. Scroll and the layers peel apart.
